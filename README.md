### Current focus

- **Epigenetic drift**: is the loss of chromatin marks *structured* or random?
- **Partial reprogramming**: OSK and chemical cocktails lower epigenetic age, but the effect fades once treatment stops. Are they genuinely resetting the epigenome, or only transiently driving a youthful transcriptional program on top of one that never changed?
- **Single-cell resolution**: bulk sequencing reports averages, which cannot say whether an intervention acts on the cells that drifted most.
